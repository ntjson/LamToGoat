## Rules that hold whichever direction wins

- **Logo:** `assets/brand/lamto-logo.png` as-is (navy `#003080` + orange `#FF7C00` on transparent).
  It only works on a light ground; on navy or orange fields half of it disappears. Always shown whole, never recoloured.
- **Screenshots:** crop and mask only. No redraws, no glow, no rotation. Scale is chosen so the UI's own body text is
  at least 28 px at 1080p. That means ≥1.0x for the 780-wide app screens, ≥1.8x for the 1440-wide web screens, and
  ≥1.1x for the @2x explorer. So web screens always appear as tight crops, and the camera pushes in to show detail.
  The explorer pair is cropped to the integrity card only (white interior at 700,1198 is 1480x300 @2x; header row is
  1480x126); the step badges below it never appear. (These figures are for the gate-1 files. For the final @3x/@4x/@5x
  set, every rect and scale lives in `docs/crops.json`.)
- **Type floor:** nothing under 28 px at 1080p. Headlines 110-140 px, labels 42-56 px, hashes 46-58 px. The message of every
  frame must survive the 360 px phone test. The UI crops' small text will not survive it, so the frame's own type carries the message.
- **Vietnamese:** text is NFC. Caps are allowed (all four fonts stack marks cleanly: Ử, Ỗ, Ễ, Ậ tested), with line-height ≥1.1
  under caps. Units and amounts never break across lines: `20.000đ/căn/tháng`, `18–34tr`, `59,2%` and `08/2027` are kept whole
  with no-break spaces where needed. Be Vietnam Pro and League Spartan have no `→` glyph, so arrows are drawn in SVG.
- **Numbers:** tabular figures; they count up on springs and always land together with a visual event, never as a table.
- **Colour meaning:** red only for tampering, alarms and over-limit figures. Green only where it appears inside the real UI.
- **Banned (from CLAUDE.md), designed out of all three:** centred title on a gradient, fade-ins as the default entrance,
  corner labels, frame borders, glow on UI, particle bursts, static walls of numbers.

Fonts are in `assets/fonts/`, all SIL OFL 1.1, all passing a full Vietnamese coverage check
(`tools/check_font_vi.py`, 146/146 letters). At gate 1 these were Be Vietnam Pro, League Spartan,
Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Mono; after the pick only Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Mono remain. Textures in `assets/textures/` are generated by
`tools/gen_textures.sh` with fixed seeds and are byte-identical on every run.

## A. Beck–Vignelli transit diagram

*After Harry Beck's London Underground diagram (1933) and Massimo Vignelli's New York subway diagram (1972).*

**Idea.** The whole film is one continuous transit map. "Tiền quỹ đi đâu?" gets answered by a line you can ride, from
a resident's report to the public ledger and back again. "Truy vết" (tracing) becomes literally following a line.
Each chapter is a new district of the same map. The last shot pulls back to show the entire route, and that frame is
the poster.

- **Palette.** Paper `#EDE8DE`, ink `#16181D`, LamTo route navy `#003080`, sealing and money orange `#FF7C00`,
  alarm red `#D8261C`, secondary text `#5E5A52`. Flat colour only.
- **Type.** Be Vietnam Pro 700 for headlines and station names, 500 for secondary labels; IBM Plex Mono 600 for hashes
  and codes. Sentence case throughout, as on the maps.
- **Texture.** None. It's a clean printed map, with the white UI crops the only other surface.
- **Geometry.** Lines 22 px, only 0°, 45° and 90°, bends of 26 px radius. Stations are ticks; interchanges are white rings.
  Termini are bars. Future and planned sections are dashed, as on real maps.
- **Camera language.** A 2D camera rides the line on springs: dolly along the path, slow down at stations, pull back at
  chapter ends to show where we are on the map. No cuts inside a chapter. Chapter changes follow the line around a bend
  into the next district.
- **Motion grammar.** Lines draw themselves (stroke on a spring). Station ticks extend and labels slide out perpendicular
  to the line. UI crops unfold out of a station like a platform, as a clip wipe from the line outwards. Numbers count
  up at termini. There is a new station or branch every 3-4 s.
- **Chapter devices.** Hook: a report station whose line dead-ends, plus a figure that changes with no station and no trace.
  Problem: each statistic is a line that runs to its share of a faint 100% line (17%, 36%, 52%), with 1.363 tòa as a
  field of station dots. Market: TAM/SAM/SOM as fare zones, with 20.000đ/căn/tháng as the fare. Who hurts: three lines
  (Ban quản trị, Ban quản lý, Cư dân), each with a disruption that clears at the LamTo interchange. Demo: the route
  Phản ánh › Xử lý › Hồ sơ bảo trì › Đề xuất chi › Thanh toán › Sổ quỹ công khai with real screens at the platforms.
  AI price band: a scale 0-18tr-34tr with the 46tr quote beyond the end of the line. Seal and tamper: the still.
  Competition: six parallel lines where only LamTo's stops at both "AI tiếp nhận" and "Sổ chống sửa đổi". Business:
  the line extends dashed through GĐ0-GĐ3, with 08/2027 as a station and 20-25 tòa at the terminus. Team: five names
  as stations, three universities as interchanges. Close: pull back to the whole map, then the logo on paper.
- **Sound pairing.** Two-tone station chimes, a soft rail rhythm in the bed, and a brake hiss when the red ring trips.
- **Risk.** It can feel cool and diagrammatic. Pace has to come from the camera, and the people chapters (ch04, ch10)
  are this direction's weakest.

## B. Isotype (Neurath–Arntz)

*After Otto Neurath and Gerd Arntz, the Vienna Method of Pictorial Statistics (Gesellschafts- und
Wirtschaftsmuseum, 1925-1934), whose charts were set in Futura.*

**Idea.** Count, don't scale. Every quantity is a row of identical pictograms: buildings, residents, disputes, coins,
ledgers. Viewers count instead of reading numbers, and the tamper is literally the odd one out.

- **Palette.** Unbleached paper `#F7EFDD`, ink `#1C1A17`, navy `#003080` for LamTo's things, orange `#FF7C00` for
  seals and money, Arntz red `#BF2E24` for disputes and tampering.
- **Type.** League Spartan 700/600 (Futura lineage); IBM Plex Mono 600 for hashes.
- **Texture.** Uncoated paper grain, seeded and multiplied over everything except the UI plates, which stay clean.
- **Pictograms.** Flat silhouettes with detail cut out in paper colour, one unit value per chart, partial pictograms for
  fractions (1.363 tòa at 100 per pictogram is 13 whole pictograms plus a 0.63 sliver, never "about 14").
- **Camera language.** Locked off and frontal, like a printed chart. Plates change with a cut or a page slide on the beat.
  The only move is a straight push onto a UI plate.
- **Motion grammar.** Pictograms stamp in one at a time (scale 1.12→1 spring plus an ink settle). Rows fill in rhythm,
  and colour flips mark the share (36 of 100 turn red). Numbers count in step with the pictograms.
- **Chapter devices.** Hook: a resident's message pictogram buried under a stack of others; a ledger figure rubbed out
  and rewritten. Problem: 1 building = 100 tòa for the 1.363, 129 of 745 turning red, 36 of 100 disputes, 52 of 100
  funds not handed over, a year of 12 months with only a few reports and no receipts. Market: nested blocks of
  apartment pictograms. Who hurts: three Arntz-style figures. Price band: coins, with the band shaded and the 46tr quote
  overflowing in red. Competition: a presence/absence matrix. Business: 500 flats × 20.000đ as bills, and buildings
  growing phase by phase.
- **Sound pairing.** A rubber-stamp thunk per pictogram, pitched by row; paper slides between plates.
- **Risk.** It's the calmest direction, so the hook needs extra help. About 20 pictograms have to be drawn in code and
  kept consistent.

## C. Saul Bass title sequence

*After Saul Bass: Anatomy of a Murder (1959), North by Northwest (1959) and his cut-paper posters.*

**Idea.** Cut-paper graphic cinema. Big flat fields, hand-cut edges, heavy condensed caps, and North by Northwest's
façade grid as the recurring motif, where every window is an apartment. The problem half plays as suspense; the
solution half moves to cream and navy fields.

- **Palette.** Orange field `#FF7C00`, cream `#F4ECDC`, black `#151311`, navy `#003080` for LamTo's truth (copies,
  verified), red `#D0271D` for tampering. The logo only ever sits on cream.
- **Type.** Bricolage Grotesque 800 at 75% width, caps, for display, with a slight seeded "hand-cut" displacement.
  Bricolage 700 for labels; IBM Plex Mono 600 for hashes.
- **Texture.** Paper grain plus fibres, with seeded displacement on every paper edge. UI is pasted up straight and
  clean with a hard black 14 px shadow.
- **Camera language.** Flat planes and hard graphic match cuts on the beat: a window becomes a phone screen, a bar becomes
  the price band. Paper shapes crossing the frame act as wipes. There is no 3D camera.
- **Motion grammar.** Shapes slide in on overshooting springs, type slams in (scale 1.08→1), and torn-paper reveals
  expose the next layer.
- **Chapter devices.** Hook: a single lit window (the complaint) slides into a façade of hundreds and is lost; a giant
  fund figure is cut out and pasted back crooked; then "TIỀN QUỸ ĐI ĐÂU?". Problem: façades whose windows turn
  orange by share. Price band: a paper bar, with the 46tr quote tearing through the frame. Competition: competitor
  names on strips that fall away. Business: a staircase of lit façades.
- **Sound pairing.** Paper slides and snaps, percussive hits, and a walking bass figure, all synthesized.
- **Risk.** It's the loudest direction and has to earn "trustworthy". It rewards bold framing but punishes clutter,
  so every frame needs one idea.

## Recommendation

**A, the Beck–Vignelli transit diagram.**

1. The product's core claim is a chain, report → decision → work → proposal → payment → public ledger. A transit
   line makes that chain visible and lets the viewer ride it in both directions, which is exactly the ch07 "trace back" beat.
2. One continuous map gives the three minutes a single camera language. Chapter transitions are motivated rather than
   decorated, and the closing pull-back doubles as `out/poster.png`.
3. It reads as public infrastructure: calm, precise, verifiable. That is the judges' takeaway we want.
4. It had the cleanest phone test (flat, high contrast, no texture). It is also the easiest to keep consistent when
   ten chapters are built in parallel, because the whole vocabulary is a few primitives: line, tick, interchange,
   terminus, label, platform.

What we give up: C has the strongest hook and B is the best at statistics and people. Within A, I'd solve the hook
with the dead-end line and the untraceable figure change, and the statistics with line-length shares and dot fields.
